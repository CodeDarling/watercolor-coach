### How to paint this subject:

**Step 1: Base color**
Apply a thin wash of neutral tint or other dark color in the dark areas (corners, crevices, openings etc.)

**Step 2: Main color**
Mix a dark-ish color from black and accent colors and apply a broader wash onto the larger metal areas. Make sure to avoid the glass areas

**Step 3: Preserve highlights + add more darks**
Repeat Step 2 to add depth in shadows, leaving the highligts to be either lighter or an accent color - e.g. bright blue or yellow, depending on the light from the lightbulb inside. 
Areas to highligt: The stems between the glass, the lower part of the top, other highlights from the surroundings

Step 4: Details
When Step 1-3 is done, use a small sized brush (eg 0, 1, 2) to add small details in the nooks and crannies in the metal. Make sure to add darker details to any holes, unless they are lit from within.

**Step 5: Adding light behind the glass**
Keep the area of the glass where the light is most intense, free from paint. In a wet wash, apply a vey thin layer of orange or yellow to darken the bright luminous light. Work in thin layers, and make sure to apply the paint to the highlighted areas on the metal. Also make sure to add highlights in the bottom areas of the lamp, depending on if it is a street lamp or handheld lantern.
