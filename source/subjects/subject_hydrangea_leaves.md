### How to paint this subject:

### How to paint this subject: Upper side of leaf

**Step 1: Highlights**
Find the highlights on the leaf and paint a very thin layer of cobalt blue or cerulean blue to mark them. Otherwise: paint around these areas when applying washes in the next steps.

**Step 2: Shadows**
Add shadows along the leaf border with botanical grey or neutral tint and in any dark crevices. Let dry. 

**Step 3: Base color**
Then, add a base light green (e.g. **Sap Green** or **Hooker's Green**) in a wash on top of the shadow areas and along the leaf veins. Let it dry.

**Step 4**
Repeat Step 2 with the same colors/wash to add more intensity of color in the already painted areas.

**Step 5**
Work in sections between veins to add more depth and color. Add more dark blue to the mix - or layer several times to achieve the correct hue. NO DETAILS YET!

**Step 6: Soften edges on veins**
After the final layers and correct values and hue have been achieved, add one final layer to the vein area to soften any edges. 

**Step 7: Texture**
To add texture to the leaf, mix a darker green than the base color, and work very lightly and with thin layers to mark the folds and valleys on the leaf, thus adding texture and dark crevices on the leaf surface. 
Move the brush from top of the vein and downwards, building layers in between drying sessions. 

**Step 8: Imperfections**
For added realism, use **Raw Umber** or **Vandyke Brown** to add small "blemishes" or dents to the leaf surface. If any areas look a bit dull or moldy, add a touch of titanium white to the areas, e.g. highlights or blemishes. 

### How to paint: the underside of the leaf

**Step 1: Shadows**
As with the upper side, add shadows to specific areas with a botanical grey or neutral tint. (Tip: along the spine in the middle of the leaf and along the outer edges on the underside)

**Step 2: Base color**
Since the underside is usually in shadow, no need for highlights. Thus, add the base color (e.g. **Terre Verte**, **Sap Green** or **Hookers Green**). 
After that, add a layer of warm green, e.g. **Green Gold** or **Transparent Yellow** to make it look warmer and lighter.

**Step 3: Intensifying the base color**
**IMPORTANT:** Make sure to avoid the veins when doing this step, to keep them lighter than the rest of the leaf. 
**Repeat Step 2** for as many times it is nessecary. Make sure to make the color richer and darker close to the "spine" in the middle of the leaf, painting the shadow areas and along the outer edges. 

**Step 4: Soften the edges and add texture**
As in Step 7 and 8 on the upside of the leaf, finish painting by applying a soft wash of lighter green on the veins to soften any sharp edges. Then pick up some brown or neutral tint, and with a tiny brush, paint small imperfections on the backside as well.
