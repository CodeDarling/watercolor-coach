### How to paint this subject:

**Step 1**
Underpainting: Underpaint the leaf with with a dark blue, like Indigo or Indanthrene blue to add depth and preliminary shadows on the leaf. Let it dry.

**Step 2**
Next layer: Use the wet-in-damp or wet-in-wet technique to add the base layer - e.g. a light sap green for the veins - only paint the areas that needs light. 

**Step 3**
Add a very very thin wash of cobalt blue (or similar) to mark the highlights on the leaf.

**Step 4**
Use the **wet-on-dry** technique and paint a light layer of dark green mix - vary the amount of blues and greens and yellows to keep it interesting.
PRESERVERVE THE VEINS AND HIGHLIGHTS!
Paint layers of the dark green mix in between drying sessions to add depth.
Paint consistency should be like tea, to avoid too much color too fast.

**Step 5**
Finishing touches: Add a darker border along the outer edge of the leaf with e.g. perylene green or a darker version of the paint mix.
