### How to paint this subject:

**Step 1: Shadows**
Add shadows to the leaves using botanical grey or Neutral Tint. 

**Step 2: Base color**
**IMPORTANT:** The veins must remain unpainted, even if they are thick.
Add a mix of Quinophtalone Yellow and Winsor Blue Red Shade on the leaves, avoiding the vein areas, and make shadows a darker or coolor green by adding more blue. 
Add a touch brown to mute the dark greens if nessecary. 

**Step 3: The veins**
Use a thin layer of Brown-Green to paint over the veins 

**Step 4: Leaf tips**
Add details to the tips of the leaf tips with a size 000 brush. Mix a dark green with a touch of brown to make them look a bit dried up as it is late summer. Remember to keep the lights light and the shadow areas dark to add contrast. 

**Step 5: Flower stem**
Use Brown-Green and Sap Green to paint the stem of the flower, adding warmt in thin washes as it fits. Add details to crevices and valleys with a size 00 or 0 brush, to add shadow and details.

**Step 6: Hairs on flower stem**
The small hairs on the flower stem are painting using all the greens and browns mixed together. Adjust the direction and angle of the hairs to make them look random and natural.
