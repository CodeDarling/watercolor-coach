### How to paint this subject:

**Step 1: Shadows**
Add shadows on the leaves with a botanical grey or neutral tint first

**Step 2: Base color**
Use a very very tiny brush (**size 0** or **1**) to apply a thin layer of light green base color 
**Mix suggestions:** Winsor Lemon, Quinophtalone Yellow mixed with Helio Green, Permanent Green or Hooker's Green. Add a touch of Quinacridone Lilac if the paint feels to bright or artificial. 
Allow a very light wash of light green to cover the thin veins that runs from the stem to the edge of the leaves.

**Step 3: Intensify colors and shadow areas**
Add more green to the darker areas on the leaves, leaving the highlights free. Add more blue or lilac to the mix to darken it. Make sure to avoid the center veins.

**Step 4: Correct mistakes**
If the middle veins have been painted over, add some finishing touches with a white paint, either guache or white watercolor (e.g. Titanium White).

Also see this video tutorial by Billy Showel: https://youtu.be/4VNFN3OYh5U?si=u8xzCybBEnhovL1f
