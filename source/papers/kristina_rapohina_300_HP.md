Kristina Rapohina's own paper from her own brand, only available on her website (see references). 
The surface is very soft and a bit rougher than traditional hotpress papers, but there is almost no drying shifts, and it's easy to achieve the light-from-within effects, especially with flowers or other delicate/thin objects. The paper can be used for detailed subjects, such as botanical paintings, animal furs, leaves, trees - all sorts of detailed work basically. The paper needs to be completely dry before adding extra layers, as the layers below will begin to dissolve.
The paper is prone to pilling though, and scrubbing is not advisable. If corrections needs to be made, it's best to use the lifting method of dapping a dry towel onto the rewet surface the needed amount of times to remove stains.
The color retention is very good though, and this paper would also work with graphite, charcoal and guache.
It's versatile but very soft surface, works best with soft and natural brush hairs, such as (Kolinsky) sable, squireel (natural or synthetic) or the like.
