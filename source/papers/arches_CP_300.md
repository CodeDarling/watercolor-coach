Ideal for any type of paints that uses granulation! This paper is the classic go-to paper for any larger subject that does not require precise details (unless the subject is zoomed-in on). The color subdues the paint, while still allowing for light to transfer through the fibers and upward, aka light from within. Price/performance ratio is in the classic end, yes, it's expensive - but very satisfying for more "rough" effects or subjects. 

It's natural white color is not the brightest to work on, but retains the light effect, allowing the paints to breathe and flow. 

Ideal subjects can be anything from dreamy, blurry and loose florals/birds/plants/skies or a stormy sea - and to metal objects such as steam trains, lanterns or steampunk items. 
It's one of the best papers for wet-in-wet techniques, as it can hold a LOT of water. It allow the paint to flow, bloom and spread out, especially if it has granulation. Ideal for loose or impressionist techniques, but a bit difficult for crisp lines using either graphite, colored pencils or brushes.
The grainy surface can take a toll on soft haired brushes, so it's advisable to check this, perhaps using a synthetic blend or pure synthetic brush, depending on the brush type/brand.
