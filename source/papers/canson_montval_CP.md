This paper is the beginner-friendly paper. It is cheap, and paints lifts easily. It's great for initial practise, and the cellulose content makes it easier to see brush strokes. 
The paper weight of 200gsm makes it easier to transfer outlines to using lightpads, but it also buckles easily. Water control is essential if using this paper. 
Light grain surface makes it suitable for almost all subjects and techniques, when the purpose is to just practise, have fun and not do serious work.

**From the manufactorer:**
Montval® paper was designed by the famous painter Gaspard Maillol, nephew of the sculptor Aristide Maillol, and manufactured by Canson® at his request in the early 20th century. Since then, this paper has acquired new qualities.  
One of them is its great erasability: corrections can be made easily, cleaning the paper with water, using a sponge or a brush. There is no trace of watercolour left and the paper can be worked again.  
Easy to use, it is accessible and suitable for everyone.
