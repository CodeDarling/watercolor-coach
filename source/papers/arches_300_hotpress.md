Ideal paper for botanical paintings, but it smells bad when water is applied onto it. 
Its quality is non-negotiable, it can take a lot of layers, it allows for a subtle glow in the colors when using it. 
It can take a LOT of layers. It's one of the papers with the absolute best performance for botanical painting when using the layering technique. 
The colors are absorbed immediately due to the cotton content, and it is great for all dry or wet techniques. 
It allows for the most precise lines achievable along with Saunders Waterford Hotpress paper. 
The smoothness of the paper's surface is almost unsurpassed by any other paper on the market, and it allows for light to pass through color, even after several layers. It can be used with or without masking fluide or tape, without any issues, and is ideal for use with a lightpad. It takes well to graphite too, or colored pencils. 
Avoid mixing colors directly on the paper's surface, as the high absorption will disturb the mixing process. 

**From the manufactor: Info about paper coating/sizing:**
Sized to the core with natural gelatin: a process exclusive to ARCHES®. ARCHES® is the only paper mill in the world to gelatin size its watercolour paper “to the core”. Even if it is soaked for a long time, the paper will still retain enough gelatin not to become too absorbent.
