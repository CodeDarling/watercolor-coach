The paper can not take as many layers as the Arches 300gsm hotpress paper. The sizing on the surface is different, which makes it difficult to work with for botanical painting. It feels like a board more than a paper, and it's not easy to cut out or transfer images onto with a lightpad either. After first experience, this paper will only be used if nothing else is available. The price/performance ratio is simply too bad, especially for the associated price tag. Sort of smelly when water is added to the surface.

Clean or crisp edges are very difficult to obtain for this paper, which is contradictory to its purpose - precise or detailed paintings. It needs considerable time to dry before new layers are applied or the pilling effects will begin to show on the surface. 
The surface itself is not as smooth as the 300gsm, but rather soft and scratchy. 

One thing it is good at though: Letting the colors glow - and it NEVER buckles.
