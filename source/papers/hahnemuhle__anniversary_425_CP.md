Suitable for masking fluid and masking tape. This paper - although very thick, has many purposes, but the heavy weight makes it difficult to use with a lightpad. It is very sturdy, and has mainly been used to try out new techniques, or used for painted color cards. Its pristine white color makes the colors pop out of the paper, as does not absorb as much paint due to the cellulose content. It behave sort of like Hahnemühle Expression paper made from 100% cotton. 
Perfect for paintings with a loose and not too precise painting style. 
Ideal for granulating paints to work optimally. 
Also usable with e.g. guache, charcoal etc. 
**Important**: Using the smoother backside increases the risk of the paper pilling if more than 1 or 2 layers are applied.

**From the manufactorer:**
A high quality watercolour board with a matt surface structure in 425 gsm. This paper quality is acid-free, lightfast and extremely resistant to ageing and suitable for all wet painting techniques such as watercolour, gouache, tempera and acrylic.
