A clear favorite, due to its budget friendly price-performance ratio and in general it's clean and solid performance. It's one of THE best papers for many detailed and precise botanical paintings, as it allows the paint to have that special, inner glow that makes the colors seem lit from within when using it. 
It's archival, UV resistant and the quality is indisguisable. It allows for a lot of scrubbing, corrections, layers and glazes, while still keeping the edges clean, firm and crisp. It lets the colors glow from within, and is very smooth to the touch. 
It absorbs colors a smidgeon slower than Arches hotpress 300gsm, and has great color absorption.
It's easy to transfer images to using a lightpad, and takes incredibly well to any type of graphite pencils, colored pencils and charcoal. 
Its surface compared to e.g. Arches hotpress 300gsm is a bit softer, as less sizing is used - so it's recommended to use a brush with softer or natural hairs, like kolinsky sable and squirrel hairs (natural and/or synthetic). 
It absorbs less color than e.g. Arches from the first washes. 
### From the manufactorer:
Saunders Waterford® is an exquisite watercolour paper, traditionally made on a cylinder mould machine. This is the superior quality watercolour paper made by St Cuthberts Mill and comes with the Royal Watercolour Society’s endorsement. Made using 100% cotton, the highest quality papermaking material, to high archival standards. Each sheet is buffered with calcium carbonate to help defend finished pieces of work from discolouration caused by acids present in atmospheric pollution.

Its attractive surface is created using natural woollen felts that give it a distinctive random texture. The surface is sized in gelatine making it strong and resilient to scrubbing, and other rough treatments.

Available in White and High White shades.
