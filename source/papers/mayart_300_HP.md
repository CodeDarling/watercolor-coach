Practise paper - not archival and not the best paper. Only available from Galleri Roholt (Private label paper)
Fine to use as a test drive or used for low-commitment subjects with less precision work. 
Too many layers will make the layers dissolve, best for practise or low-value efforts to have fun with. Takes well to graphite, easy to transfer subjects to with a lightpad or other. Also ideal for e.g. colored pencil practises.
Due to the 100% cotton content, colors seems to be absorbed or sucked into the layers, due to the soft sizing on the surface. Works impressively well with black ink!
It's not ideal for subjects that require a lot of layers, as the paint below will start to dissolve after too many layers or glazes.
