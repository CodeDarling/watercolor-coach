The first and only hotpress cellulose paper owned. Very smooth surface, easy to obtain clean, crisp and clear edges, but too many layers will make the paint below dissolve if not completely dry due to the cellulose content and low absorption quality of this paper. 
Colors pops and appear bright, it is very white and allows the light to pass through. 
It takes extremely well to graphite and colored pencils though, and is usable with both masking fluid and masking tape, as well as lightpad. 
The cellulose content shows brush lines more readily, and is not ideal for e.g. botanical watercolor subjects.
It is fun to play with, when the purpose is not too serious, or used for mixed media effects. Will probably work well with acrylics, but not watercolor!
