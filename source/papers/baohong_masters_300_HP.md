One of the newer papers on the market - only available from Aart De Vos (online) in DK. The paper is very hard sized on the surface and smooth - but not as smooth as Arches HP 300gsm. Still, it holds up to a bit of layering before the layers below begin to dissolve. It does not smell when water is added to the surface.
It has a high degree of whiteness, just as Saunders Extra White, but it feels rather thin to the touch. It allows for light to pass through from below to a quite satisfying degree, better than Arches hotpress 300gsm. 
It's ideal for detailed or botanical subjects, and takes well to masking fluid. It's easy to transfer subjects to using a lightpad, and takes well to graphite or colored pencils. 

### From the manufactor:
Baohong Masters’ has a unique slow absorption rate, allowing artists more time to manipulate colour and pigment. This property aligns with traditional Chinese painting techniques that require precise control over colour flow. The paper’s controlled absorbency allows for the most subtle gradations essential in delicate watercolour painting techniques.

 - Sizing: A meticulous sizing process, both internal and external, using traditional gelatine, was crucial for controlling paint flow and for preventing buckling. 
- Performance: The paper readily accepts multiple washes without buckling or pilling, allowing for seamless blending and lifting techniques. 
- Longevity: The Acid-free and pH neutral paper is designed to withstand the test of time, preserving artwork for generations.
